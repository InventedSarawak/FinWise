A money coach that learns from your own spending

Trackers record what you spent. FinWise also explains it, at the moment it matters.

1

Give consent

Connect your UPI and bank accounts through a consent-based flow. We read amounts, dates and merchants. We never move money or see your PIN, and you can pause or delete access any time.

2

See your safe-to-spend

One number for today, built around your monthly allowance and the days left in the month, with a clear view of where the rest went.

3

Learn when it matters

Add a third subscription or get a credit offer, and a 2-minute lesson shows up with a quick check, so the idea sticks.

Practise credit before it costs you

Try a practice card with virtual money. In our example, an ₹8,000 purchase costs ₹3,067 in interest when you pay only the minimum, and ₹777 when you pay ₹2,000 a month.

  • Virtual money only, so nothing real is at risk
  • Optional parent summary that you choose to share, every Sunday
  • Built around a student allowance, not a salary
